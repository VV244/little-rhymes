ROOTED IN REAL WORDS

Stories with a source.

Poetry carries a little piece of the world. We preserve the original words, credit their origins and keep editorial care at the centre.

Our editorial promise.

“Sourced classic” means the displayed selection has been compared with a named source and the underlying text’s rights reviewed. Selections are labelled as excerpts. It does not mean a school curriculum endorsement or a live popularity ranking.

Folk rhymes can have many authentic versions. We name the version we use. Original AI demonstrations remain clearly separate. Modern arrangements, illustrations, translations and recordings require their own permission.

Fresh content, carefully.

Wikisource’s public API checks only explicitly approved literary pages. Responses are cached for six hours and requests are paced. Every new revision becomes an editorial candidate, never an automatically published poem.

Daily rhyme is a date-based rotation of published, sourced poems. Dates below show actual successful checks. “Not checked yet” means exactly that.

Gutenberg and institutional references are manual citations unless an approved integration is explicitly listed. No paid poetry or voice API is configured.
